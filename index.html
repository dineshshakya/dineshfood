FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this a real ordering service yet?

Not yet — dineshfood is in development. The demo app shows the product vision. Join the waitlist to be notified at launch.

Who can cook on dineshfood?

Home chefs with a valid San Diego County MEHKO (Microenterprise Home Kitchen Operation) permit. We verify every permit before a kitchen goes live.

How does dineshfood make money?

A single 10% service fee on each order. Chefs keep the rest.

Where are you launching?

San Diego, starting with 92108 and surrounding neighborhoods.
