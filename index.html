FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the food safe to eat?

Yes. Home chefs follow food-safety practices, every dish carries public community ratings, and admins review each complaint — chefs with repeated issues are removed from the platform.

How do I pay for my order?

You pay securely when you order, and chefs receive their earnings through weekly payouts.

Is it delivery or pickup?

Each chef sets their own options — many offer neighborhood delivery, and pickup is always available.

How do home chefs join?

Sign up, post your menu, and switch your availability on. You receive orders, cook fresh, and track everything from your own dashboard.

When will it launch in my neighborhood?

We are onboarding area by area. Join the waitlist below and we will reach out as soon as your zip code opens.
